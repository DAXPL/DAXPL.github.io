Poznań Game Arena 2021

Organizing the Propaganda Studios booth at the largest gaming fair in Poland.

About the Event

Poznań Game Arena 2021 was the first major opportunity for us, as Propaganda Studios, to present our games to a wide audience. Over the three days of the fair, our booth was visited by hundreds of players, media representatives, and other developers. It was an amazing experience to see people playing something we created from scratch. Our main showcased title was "Poszutex" – a game created during the Global Game Jam (awarded at the "Kombajn Game Jam"), where the player searches for lost items in locations based on real places.

My Contribution and Responsibilities

As a co-founder of Propaganda Studios and one of the main programmers, my contribution was not limited just to code. I was actively involved in every aspect of our presence at the fair:

  • Organization and Logistics: I coordinated the preparation of hardware.
  • Presentation and feedback collection: I was one of the main people at the booth – actively inviting guests to play, explaining mechanics, and most importantly, noting down invaluable feedback from dozens of players.
  • Game updating: With such a large number of players, bugs were coming to light almost every hour. After returning from the fair, I spent nights analyzing the collected feedback and patching the found bugs so that the next morning, players could test a newer, more stable version of the game.
