Where it all started

Participation in a scientific project involving 3D printing of the skulls of the first inhabitants of Greater Poland for anthropological reconstruction.

About the Event

This was a large-scale, interdisciplinary scientific project "Where it all started", co-financed by the EU and implemented by the Archaeological Museum, among others, aiming to present the beginnings of Polish statehood on Ostrów Tumski in a modern way. The project involved creating reconstructions of the appearance of the first inhabitants of the stronghold (buried in the cemetery in Śródka), which were used to create modern holograms, among other things.

The Complex of Electrical Schools No. 2, as part of the GITES science club, was invited to a crucial stage: preparing physical 3D models of skulls, which served as a basis for further anthropological and artistic work.

My Contribution and Responsibilities

As an active member of the GITES group, I participated in the work of the team led by Prof. Sebastian Giedziun. My role consisted of technical support for the 3D printing process and active promotion of our work's results.

  • 3D Printing (FDM): I assisted in the preparation and operation of FDM 3D printers. This allowed us to reduce the cost by 15 times compared to the originally used CJP technology.
  • Project Presentation: Together with the GITES team, I actively participated in a scientific meeting in the Session Room of the City of Poznań, organized by the Society of Lovers of the City of Poznań. We supported Prof. Giedziun's presentation "How to see the faces of the first inhabitants of Poznań?".
  • Technical Support: I helped operate the equipment (provided by Accura, among others) to ensure the high quality and precision of the prints necessary for scientific purposes.
