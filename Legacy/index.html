About Me

My serious journey with technology began in technical school, where I received the Golden Laurel award for my activity in the Gites group – an award that reminds me that hard work makes anything possible. That's where I learned the most important lesson: true motivation comes from passion. I stick to this principle to this day, which allows me to combine work with what I love.

My greatest passion is gamedev and XR technologies (VR/AR/MR). The weirder the technology, the better! I founded my own studio, Propaganda Studios, and the Errno science club, which won the title of the best science club at Adam Mickiewicz University in the scientific category. From LabVR projects for schools, through teaching AI to navigate a boat, to creating games at game jams or even programming firearm overlays – I am not afraid of challenges, because every potential failure is an experience that I forge into my next success.

Featured Projects

SNS Minerwa

Minerwa is a virtual shooting range developed for the ZSE2 technical school, allowing for safe shooting practice in a school environment using a projector and a rifle replica. The heart of the project is an innovative machine vision system. Instead of expensive lasers or IR transmitters, a small camera is mounted on the barrel. The image from it is analyzed by an algorithm that tracks special markers on the screen, allowing for precise shot accuracy calculation.
