As co-founder and vice-president of the **Errno Student Science Club** at the Faculty of Physics, Adam Mickiewicz University, I co-directed the organization of **Pestka Jam** — an on-site, 72-hour non-stop game development sprint.

The event gathered creators spanning programming (Unity, Godot), 2D/3D visual art, game design, and audio composition.

---

## Organizer & Technical Mentor Roles

Managing a 72-hour hackathon requires extensive logistical and technical oversight:

* **Facilities & Logistics:** Establishing reliable low-latency networks, power distribution, and round-the-clock workstations for dozens of participants across three days.
* **On-Floor Technical Mentorship:** Providing continuous architectural consulting in Unity, troubleshooting rendering bottlenecks, and assisting teams with aggressive scope trimming to meet submission deadlines.
* **Showcase Coordination:** Directing the concluding live showcase stream, facilitating Q&A panels, and coordinating structured judge evaluations.

## Takeaways
Fast-paced hackathons and game jams are unmatched laboratories for learning engineering trade-offs. Participants test their technical resilience under strict time limits, while mentoring provides a fulfilling avenue to transfer production practices to emerging creators.
