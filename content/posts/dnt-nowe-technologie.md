**New Technology Days (DNT)** is one of the premier educational and community technology festivals in Poznań, celebrating hands-on engineering, embedded electronics, game development, and immersive realities.

I have had the privilege of co-organizing **every edition to date** across 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 — initially as a member and leader of the GITES research circle at ZSE2, and later in partnership with the Faculty of Physics and Astronomy, Adam Mickiewicz University.

---

## Festival Evolution & Activities

Over the years, my role evolved from managing individual tech demo stands to directing entire workshop tracks and logistics:

* **Practical Workshops:** Designing and conducting interactive modules on FDM 3D printing, C# foundations, and introductory Unity VR environments.
* **Flagship Project Demos:** Presenting the *Minerwa* virtual marksmanship system, cooperative VR space simulators, and autonomous *WST* drone platforms to visitors of all ages.
* **Secondary-to-Higher Education Pipeline:** Actively bridging high school technical students with university science circles, providing young engineers with early onramps to real-world projects.

## Organizational Takeaways
Sustained involvement with DNT over many years demonstrated the incredible momentum generated by passionate, purpose-driven technical communities. It provided extensive experience in large-scale event coordination, media communications, and public scientific outreach.
