**Poznań Game Arena 2021** was the first major opportunity for us at **Propaganda Studios** to showcase our games to a broad public audience. Across three packed convention days, our booth in the Indie Zone was visited by hundreds of players, media representatives, and fellow game creators.

Seeing people dive into a title we built from scratch was an incredible experience. Our headline game was **"Poszutex"** – created during Global Game Jam (and awarded at "Kombajn Game Jam"), where players search for misplaced items across levels inspired by real Polish locations.

---

## My Contribution & Responsibilities

As co-founder of Propaganda Studios and one of our lead programmers, my duties extended far beyond writing code:

* **Logistics & Hardware Management:** Coordinated hardware inventory, wiring, and station setups.
* **On-Floor Presentation & Live Feedback:** Actively guided visitors into gameplay, explained core mechanics, and recorded observations on user friction and UX bottlenecks.
* **Overnight Patching & Hotfixes:** High player throughput surfaced unexpected edge cases almost hourly. Each evening after the exhibition closed, I spent nights analyzing user logs and shipping hotfixes so attendees had a smoother build the next morning.

## Key Takeaways
* **Direct Assumption Testing:** Nothing tests design intuitions like watching an absolute beginner's first 30 seconds of interaction.
* **Rapid Deployment Discipline:** Shipping patches under tight convention turnarounds reinforced good version control habits and defensive architecture.
* **Industry Networking:** Connecting with fellow Polish indie studios fueled future collaborations and game jams.
