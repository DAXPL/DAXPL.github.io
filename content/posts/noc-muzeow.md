As part of the nationwide **Museum Night** celebration, I conducted a series of interactive technical demonstrations and public lectures at the Faculty of Physics, AMU, organized with the **Fantastyczny UAM** science club.

---

## Program & Community Engagement

The objective was demystifying modern physics, computer science, and spatial computing for a diverse public audience of all age groups:

* **Hands-On XR Stations:** Giving visitors direct hands-on time with virtual reality simulations, observing firsthand how graphics engines model optical and physical phenomena.
* **Embedded IoT & 3D Prototyping Demos:** Displaying real hardware prototypes powered by ESP32 microcontrollers alongside precision anatomical 3D prints.
* **Public Technology Discussions:** Engaging attendees in discussions concerning the trajectory of immersive hardware, simulation ethics, and open-source tooling in modern education.
