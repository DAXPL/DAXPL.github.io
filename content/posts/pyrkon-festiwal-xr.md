During both the 2025 and 2026 editions of the **Pyrkon International Festival of Fantastic** — one of the largest pop-culture conventions in Europe — I actively participated as an exhibitor in close collaboration with the **Fantastyczny UAM** university science club.

---

## Interactive Mixed Reality (MR) Zone

The focal point of our booth was introducing thousands of festival attendees to spatial computing and Mixed Reality workflows:

* **Passthrough & Spatial Computing Experiences:** Showcasing proprietary applications leveraging high-resolution color passthrough on Meta Quest headsets, seamlessly anchoring digital simulations and physical interactions onto convention grounds.
* **STEM & Educational Outreach:** Demonstrating educational applications — ranging from celestial astrophysics scale models to interactive hardware engineering anatomy.
* **Fleet Management & Technical Support:** Managing continuous operation of multiple standalone VR/MR headsets under massive attendee throughput, maintaining strict hygiene protocols and guiding first-time spatial computing users.
