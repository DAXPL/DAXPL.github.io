### 🖨️ Fabrication & Rapid Prototyping
* **Ender 3 3D Printer (or rather 3 and 3/4):** An original 2018 Ender 3, modified so extensively that my friends and I often debate whether it even still qualifies as an Ender 3. Bought as a temporary solution, it remains virtually indestructible.
  * **Mainboard:** MKS Robin Nano V3.1
  * **Z-Axis:** Dual Z-axis lead screws
  * **Bed:** Glass build plate
  * **Cooling:** [Satsana Dual 5015 Fan Duct](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5750189) with a 4020 fan
  * **Extruder:** [Direct Drive](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5396802) conversion
  * **Print Host:** DELL WYSE 3040 running OctoPrint on DietPi, paired with the cheapest Logitech webcam I bought around 2018
* **Filament Dryer:** CREALITY Space Pi
* **Soldering Station:** Yihua 878D 700W (Iron + Hot-Air rework)

### 💻 Workstation & Environment
* **Primary Workstation:** Built for Unity project compilation, machine learning model training, and VR rendering:
  * **CPU:** AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D
  * **GPU:** RTX 4070 SUPER
  * **RAM:** GOODRAM IRDM 64GB [2x32GB 6400MHz DDR5 CL32 DIMM] tuned to 6000 MT/s
  * **Motherboard:** Gigabyte X670 GAMING X AX V2
  * **CPU Cooler:** Noctua NH-D15 chromax.black — because I'm done dealing with failing pumps in AiO liquid coolers
  * **Power Supply:** be quiet! Pure Power 12 M 1000W
  * **Case:** SilentiumPC Aquarius AQ-X70W Pure Black — the greatest PC case in the world, and nobody will convince me otherwise
  * **OS:** Windows 11 Edu with Chris Titus Tech's [winutil](https://github.com/christitustech/winutil) script
  * **Keyboard:** Dark Project ALU Midnight — the moment I realized I'd never be as good at Blender as Emil is blindfolded with both hands tied behind his back, I ditched the numpad in favor of great tactile clicks, a DETACHABLE CABLE, and web-based configuration software
  * **Mouse:** Logitech G305
* **Servers & Homelab:**
  * **DELL WYSE 5070:** Small server hosting HAOS (Home Assistant OS), n8n, and whichever services I need at any given moment
  * **Linux (Debian / DietPi / Arch):** Powering homelab servers, firmware compilation, and containerization
