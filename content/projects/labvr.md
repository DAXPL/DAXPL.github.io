**LAB-VR** is an interactive virtual reality simulation of specialized classroom laboratories engineered for primary and secondary education. The project brings traditional curriculum lessons into VR, turning abstract concepts into hands-on experiences.

The application was funded under the prestigious Polish **Kowadło 2.0** educational innovation grant and released entirely as free **open-source software**, providing schools across the country with a modern teaching toolkit.

---

## My Role in the Project

As **Project Leader and Lead Unity Developer**, I oversaw the entire software development lifecycle:
* **Team Leadership & Mentoring:** Directing developers from the Student Science Club Errno while coordinating internships for vocational IT students, exposing them to industry-standard gamedev practices.
* **VR Architecture & Mechanics:** Architecting and implementing physics-based hand interaction mechanics within Unity 3D.
* **Standalone Mobile Optimization:** Profiling performance for mobile Snapdragon XR2 chipsets (Meta Quest 2), securing a consistent 72/90 FPS without tethering to a PC.

---

## Educational Subjects & Laboratories

The simulation features dedicated virtual classrooms offering experiments that are otherwise dangerous or cost-prohibitive in standard school labs:

* **Physics:** Perform mechanics, optics, and electromagnetism experiments with real-time physical vector visualizations.
* **Astronomy:** Scale planetary models, explore celestial systems, and inspect orbital trajectories from first- and third-person viewpoints.
* **Geology:** Virtual field expeditions dissecting Earth's cross-sections, identifying mineral samples, and examining tectonic movements.
* **Computer Hardware Engineering:** A space where PC hardware comes to life — students disassemble motherboards, inspect bus architectures, and learn computer engineering interactively.

---

## Nationwide School Workshops & Rollout

Democratizing access to XR technologies is a core pillar of LAB-VR. We hosted hands-on workshops across multiple educational institutions in Poland:

* **Sep 8, 2023:** ZSE2 Technical High School in Poznań – initial field validation and dev workshops.
* **Dec 8, 2023:** Tischner Technical High School in Rabka-Zdrój – pilot rollout and developer talks.
* **Feb 22, 2024:** Mieszko I Technical Complex in Stargard – classroom deployment and student mentoring.
* **Apr 12, 2024:** Elementary School in Gołańcz – interactive STEM workshop for primary school students.
