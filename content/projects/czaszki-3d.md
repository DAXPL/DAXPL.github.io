**"Where It All Started"** (*"Tu się wszystko zaczęło"*) was a large-scale, EU-co-financed research initiative conducted in partnership with the Archaeological Museum in Poznań and the *Genius Loci* Archaeological Reserve.

The project aimed to deliver a modern, multimedia perspective on the origins of Polish statehood on Ostrów Tumski island. A primary goal was reconstructing the physical appearance of the region's earliest medieval settlers (unearthed at the historical Śródka burial ground) to generate cutting-edge museum holographic displays.

The Complex of Electrical Schools No. 2 (ZSE2 in Poznań), through the **GITES** school research club, was invited to undertake a vital engineering milestone: fabricating tangible, high-precision 3D anatomical skull reproductions.

## My Contribution & Responsibilities

As an active member of the GITES research circle under the direction of Prof. Sebastian Giedziun, my work involved:
* **FDM 3D Printing:** Assisting in model slicing, infill parameter tuning, and daily machine park operation.
* **Public Presentation:** Presenting our engineering workflow alongside our instructors at the official scientific session held in the City of Poznań Session Hall (*"How to see the faces of Poznań's first inhabitants?"*).
* **Equipment Calibration:** Calibrating professional 3D printing machines (provided in part by Accura) to meet the strict micrometer tolerances needed by forensic anthropologists.

---

## Engineering Impact

### 1. 15x Cost Reduction via FDM Process Engineering
Original project estimates were pegged to industrial ColorJet Printing (CJP), which was extremely cost-prohibitive. Working with the team, we substituted this with an optimized **Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM)** workflow:
* By tailoring layer heights, infill densities, and organic support geometries to intricate anatomical sinus and bone structures, we maintained full forensic fidelity.
* This procedural shift **reduced unit production costs fifteen-fold (15x)** compared to CJP powder printing, securing the project's financial viability.

### 2. Science Communication at Poznań City Hall
The project demanded clear technical communication. We represented the team at the Poznań City Council Session Hall under the auspices of the Society of Lovers of the City of Poznań. We demonstrated our 3D models to municipal authorities, museum curators, and media representatives, answering detailed technical inquiries.

## Key Achievements
* **Applied R&D:** Practical deployment of additive manufacturing for historical and anthropological science.
* **15-Fold Cost Reduction:** Overcame budgetary bottlenecks through methodical slicing and printer optimization.
* **Dimensional Accuracy:** Maintained forensic standards required for subsequent facial soft-tissue sculpting.
